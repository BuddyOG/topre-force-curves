Build against every part and dome ever made, with compatibility checked live from one confirmed dataset — stem fit, ring preload, top-out chatter, drilling requirements, unreleased-part warnings. Measured, not marketed.

Every dome is sold as one number; your fingers feel the whole curve. The Force Curves tool overlays bench-measured curves for every tested dome and configuration and compares them by Total Energy (how heavy it really is) and Snap % (how tactile). Dome-to-dome variation is first-class: supposedly identical domes measure wildly differently, and the library shows it.

See the switch work.

The Workshop is a working cross-section of the electrostatic capacitive switch, driven by your build: swap sliders, fit rings, watch preload, travel, and the collapse point move on real measured behavior. Drag it to press it.
