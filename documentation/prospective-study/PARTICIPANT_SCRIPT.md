# Participant script

Study ID: `perception-validation-v1`

Read the quoted text exactly. Do not add product information or expected
results.

## Before practice

> You will compare 25 electrocapacitive keyboard domes. The domes are coded so
> you will not know their names, advertised weights, or test results.
>
> You will give two separate whole-number scores from 1 through 10.
>
> Weight is 1 for feather-lightest in this panel and 10 for heaviest in this
> panel.
>
> Tactility sharpness is 1 for linear or no distinct collapse and 10 for the
> sharpest collapse in this panel.
>
> A dome can be heavy without being sharp, or sharp without being heavy. There
> are no right answers. Rate what you feel; do not try to identify the product.
>
> You may press each presented dome up to five times. After we move to the next
> dome, you cannot return to an earlier one. You may request a break at any
> time.
>
> The first pass is practice and will not be analyzed. Do you understand that
> Weight and Tactility sharpness are two separate scores?

If the participant says no, repeat only the two definitions and the sentence
explaining that heavy and sharp are separate. Do not give an example dome or a
suggested rating.

## Before scored session 1

> Practice is complete. After a five-minute break, you will rate the same panel
> in a new order. These scores will be recorded for the study. Please use the
> same definitions, but do not try to reproduce a practice score from memory.

## Before scored session 2

> This is the second scored session. The order is different. Use what you feel
> now. Do not try to remember or reproduce your earlier scores.

## Every scored trial

Ask only:

> Weight, from 1 to 10?

Then:

> Tactility sharpness, from 1 to 10?

If the answer is not a whole number from 1 through 10, say:

> Please choose one whole number from 1 through 10.

## Breaks

> Would you like a short break before continuing?

Do not discuss domes or ratings during a break.

## End of a session

> That session is complete. Thank you. Please do not discuss the coded domes,
> their order, or your scores with someone who may participate later.
