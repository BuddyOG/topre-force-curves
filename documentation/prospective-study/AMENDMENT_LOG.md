## Rules

- A spelling or formatting correction that cannot affect conduct, data, or
  interpretation may be recorded as administrative.
- Any change to the apparatus, panel, participant instructions, session
  timing, practice, press limit, randomization, outcomes, exclusion rules,
  sample threshold, hypotheses, or analysis is substantive.
- Before enrollment, a substantive change requires a new version, updated
  manifest, new Git tag, and a dated explanation.
- After enrollment, never erase the original rule. Add a dated amendment,
  state which participants or trials it affects, and report both the original
  and amended analysis where relevant.
- No amendment may be justified by whether a result agrees with the pilot or
  released indices.
