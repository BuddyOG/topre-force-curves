Research publication: Beyond Snap Ratio Preprint 2.1 is available at doi:10.5281/zenodo.22295223; doi:10.5281/zenodo.22167064 resolves to the latest version. Its research compendium is at doi:10.5281/zenodo.22167047. The prospective multi-rater protocol is frozen as perception-validation-v1 but has not yet produced participant evidence.
Published EC Parts Builder: The lib-6.1 release has 11 component rows, 28 keyboard starters, 4 manufacturer-parts shortcuts, 10 recorded 2u assemblies, 68 exact specimen measurements, and 241 decision-changing browser pair edges; the complete 3,570-edge configuration remains the audit authority. Compatibility labels include Works, Works with conditions, Does not work, Not verified, and the declared Manufacturer matched fallback; domes are Not evaluated.
Product boundary: Shopify owns the Dome Lab home, header, and navigation. The generated EC Parts Builder is one standalone tool with contextual catalog choosers, no in-tool tabs or master library page, and no links to other tools. The lib-6.1 release overlay preserves the fc-3.4 viewer, raw files, canonical evidence, and continuity endpoints under the explicit manifest policy.
